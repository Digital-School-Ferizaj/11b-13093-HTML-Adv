What is Global Warming?

Global warming is the gradual increase in Earth's average temperature. It is mainly caused by greenhouse gases released through human activities such as burning fossil fuels, cutting down forests, and industrial pollution. Climate change affects ecosystems, weather patterns, and human life around the world.

Why Should We Care?

Climate change impacts every country in the world. Rising temperatures cause stronger storms, floods, droughts, melting ice caps, and rising sea levels. By understanding the problem and taking action, we can create a healthier and more sustainable future.
