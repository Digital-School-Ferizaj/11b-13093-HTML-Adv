Start with a local action

Climate change is a global issue, but many useful activities begin in schools and neighborhoods.

  • Organize a classroom energy-saving week.
  • Plant and care for native trees.
  • Create posters about reducing food and plastic waste.
  • Walk, cycle or use shared transport when possible.
  • Research how climate change could affect your region.

Learn first. Discuss clearly. Act consistently.
