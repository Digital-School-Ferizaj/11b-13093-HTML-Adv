What is happening?

Earth is warming because more heat is being trapped in the atmosphere.

Global warming is the long-term rise in Earth’s average surface temperature. Human activities, especially burning coal, oil and gas, add greenhouse gases to the atmosphere and strengthen the natural greenhouse effect.

  • Temperatures and heatwaves are increasing.
  • Glaciers and ice sheets are losing ice.
  • Sea levels are rising and weather patterns are changing.

Visible consequences

Global warming affects natural and human systems

The effects are connected. A warmer atmosphere and ocean can influence water, ecosystems, food production, health and cities.

Drought and water stress

Hotter conditions can dry soils faster and increase pressure on water supplies.

Flooding and heavy rain

Changing rainfall patterns can increase flooding risks in many communities.

Extreme heat

More frequent and intense heat can affect health, energy use and daily life.

What can help?

Solutions already exist

Reducing greenhouse gas emissions requires action from governments, businesses, communities and individuals. Clean energy, efficient buildings, better transport and forest protection are all part of the solution.

Clean energySolar and wind power can replace fossil fuel electricity.
EfficiencyUsing less energy reduces both emissions and costs.
NatureHealthy forests and ecosystems store carbon and support life.
